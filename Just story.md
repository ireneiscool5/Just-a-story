Twenty Years, Unsaid
I'm twenty-two, and I don't think I ever got the chance to be a kid.
My grandfather was shot. Someone took the person who might have been my softest place to land. After that, nobody sat down with me and asked how I was holding up. Grief came, and I had to carry it quietly.
My family doesn't hold me the way I hoped. I look around at people my age who seem to have grown up with someone in their corner, and I feel years behind. Mostly I've just felt down, for so long that it started to feel like my normal.
